The weighted least squares fit typically reduces the heteroscedasticity metrics. On a weighted fit the tests are computed from the standardized residuals, the raw residuals multiplied by the square root of their weights, so a drop in the statistics means that the weighting worked.

5. Check the variance function

fitWLS() assumed that the log of the error variance is linear in the regressors. performVarianceFormTest() tests that shape against the squares and products of the same regressors.

performVarianceFormTest(wls_model)
## 
##  Specification test of the variance function (exponential form)

A small p-value says that the variance function needs those extra terms, and that another form or var_formula in fitWLS() is worth trying. Unlike the tests in step 4, this one allows for the weights having been estimated from the same data.
